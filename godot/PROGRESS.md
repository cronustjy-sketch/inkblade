# Progress

Build log for the Godot remake. Each entry says what was done and how to test it.
Run the project with F5 (main scene). Debug keys (editor runs only): F1 win, F2 lose, F3 hero run, F4 hero slash.

- **Step 1: battle scene and castles.** Lane, two ink-style castles with HP bars, camera. Test: F5, hold ← → to pan.
- **Step 2: unit data and AI.** `data/units.gd` holds all stats; units walk, fight, die. Test: spawn swordsmen and watch them fight.
- **Step 3: HUD and enemy spawner.** Energy bar, pop counter, spawn buttons, timed enemy waves. Test: click the swordsman button once energy >= 20.
- **Step 4: win/lose screen.** Destroy a castle (or press F1/F2) to get Victory/Defeat with Next Level / Retry. Test: press F1, click Next Level.
- **Step 6: stickmen and hero model.** Units are ink-style 3D stickmen with weapons (`scripts/stickman.gd`); the hero uses `hero_anim.glb`. Test: set `start_level` to 10 on the Battle node and watch all unit types; F3/F4 preview hero animations.
  (Built before step 5 at the user's request; committed together with step 4.)
- **Step 5: hero.** The hero (`scripts/hero.gd`, model `hero_anim.glb`) moves with ← → (or A/D), auto-attacks the nearest enemy or castle in range (every 3rd hit is a ×2.2 crit), can die and respawns after 5 s. **Q** Slash hits everything within 7 m in front for 70. Camera follows the hero. HUD has a skill bar bottom-right. Hero and skill stats live in `data/units.gd`. Test: F5, move with ← →, press Q next to enemies. `scenes/dev_smoke.tscn` is a developer smoke test (run headless: `godot --headless --path . res://scenes/dev_smoke.tscn`).
- **Step 7: unit specials.** Archers shoot arrows (shield knights in front soak them), witches and catapults throw splash projectiles (catapult ×2 vs castles), spearmen ×2 vs big units, shield knights take 35% less damage, assassins crit 30% (×2.5), priests heal the lowest-HP ally for 16, giants hit everything near the target and knock it back, cavalry's first hit is ×2 with knockback, dragons fly (ground melee can't hit them). Data lives in `data/units.gd`, code in `unit.gd` / `projectile.gd`. Test: set `start_level` to 10 on the Battle node and watch the mixed armies; `dev_smoke.tscn` checks all of these headless.
- **Step 8: orders and take-control.** Bottom-left buttons (or keys 1/2/3): Attack pushes forward, Defend holds a line 7 m in front of your castle (still fights what comes in range), Retreat walks everyone into the castle where they heal 8% max HP/s and can't be attacked. Click a soldier (or Tab) to control it with ← → / A D: it deals +40% damage, a gold marker shows above it, the camera follows it. H or clicking the hero returns control to the hero. Test: spawn soldiers, click one, move it into the enemy; try the three orders.
- **Step 9: hero skills and the ultimate.** **E** Dash (unlocks level 2): fast dash forward, 40 damage to each enemy passed, invincible 0.45 s. **R** Rally (level 4): army heals 30% and attacks ~45% faster for 6 s. **F** Sword Rain (level 6): swords fall on 9 m ahead for 2.2 s. **G** Ink Vortex (level 12): hero's own skill, damages and shoves everything within 5 m. **Space** (or the button): 墨刃·终 Ink Blade Final, a screen-wide strike once the ink meter is full; the meter fills from damage dealt (hero ×4 more than soldiers). Test: set `start_level` to 12 on the Battle node to have every skill; fight until the ink bar fills, then press Space.
- **Step 10: levels 1-20, bosses and the level-select map.** The game now starts on the map (`scenes/map.tscn`): 20 stops along an ink path, progress saved to `user://progress.cfg`, credit "Junyuan" under the title. Bosses (`data/bosses.gd`): L5 石拳首领 950/26, L10 草原霸主 1900/34, L15 熔岩巨魔 3600/46, L20 墨刃魔王 6000/58. They spawn 3 s into the level with a boss bar at the top, and the enemy castle is warded (purple dome, no damage) until the boss dies. Boss models: colossus / warden / faceless (animated) and ender (static, bobs). Levels 11-20 use lava terrain. Win saves progress; the result screen has Next Level / Retry / Map. Test: pick level 5 on the map, survive 3 s, see the boss and the ward; hit the castle (nothing happens), kill the boss, then the castle takes damage. In editor runs the map has an "Unlock all (debug)" toggle. F1/F2 (instant win/lose) still exist in editor runs only. Developer tools: `scenes/dev_smoke.tscn` (headless logic checks, 100+ asserts) and `scenes/dev_preview.tscn` (lineup of every unit on a lava boss level; set env `INK_SHOT=<png>` to save a screenshot).
- **Upgrade 1: sprite characters.** All ten army units are now bold ink-brush sprite warriors generated with Codex (swordsman, archer, spearman, shield knight, witch, assassin, priest, giant, cavalry, catapult), 4 walk + 4 attack frames each, billboarded to face the camera and flipped to the walking direction. Gold-trimmed for the player, red-trimmed for the enemy (the bake tool shifts gold hues to red). The raw Codex sheets are in `art_src/raw/` (not imported by Godot); `tools/bake_sprites.gd` cuts, cleans and recolours them into `sprites/` and writes `data/sprite_meta.gd`. Sizes per unit are `sprite_h` in `data/units.gd`. The hero and bosses keep their 3D models; the dragon keeps the primitive stickman (no sprite requested). To re-bake: `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/bake_sprites.gd` then `--import`.
- **Upgrade 2 + 3: look and camera overhaul.** (a) Debug "eyes": in editor runs **F12** saves `screenshots/shot_<time>.png`; with env `INK_AUTOSHOT=1` (and optional `INK_LEVEL=<n>`, `INK_ZOOM=<z>`) the battle stages a fight around the hero, waits 5 s, saves `screenshots/battle_L<level>.png` and quits, e.g. `INK_AUTOSHOT=1 INK_LEVEL=9 godot --path . res://scenes/battle.tscn`. (b) Camera is much closer (about 20 m of lane, units ~15-20% of screen height) and follows the hero. Mouse wheel zooms (hero close-up to the whole lane), hold right mouse and drag to tilt, pinch on touch screens, **C** resets. (c) Ink-wash shan shui backdrop in three parallax layers (far / mid / near, generated with Codex, `art_src/raw_bg/` -> `backgrounds/` via `tools/bake_backdrops.gd`), with a dark-rock-and-lava-glow version for levels 11-20; the ground fades into the sky colour so mountains rise out of mist. (d) Calm ground: grass tufts, brush lines and most rocks removed. (e) The castle is now a Codex-generated ink illustration (gold for you, red for the enemy). (f) The hero is bigger and has a gold glow, a gold ground ring and a flowing gold scarf. (g) HUD rebuilt as ink panels with gold borders and Chinese titles with English underneath: castle HP bars at the top corners, level in the middle, and one bottom bar with Orders | Army (energy, pop, unit buttons with sprite portraits) | Hero Skills with the ultimate bar inside it.
- **Art restyle: classic stickman war look.** All units are pure black stickmen identified by their weapon plus a gold (player) or red (enemy) headband, with 6-frame walk and 6-frame attack animations generated with Codex: swordsman, archer, spearman, shield knight, witch, assassin, priest, giant, cavalry (stickman on a black horse), catapult (pushed by two stickmen). The hero is a slightly bigger stickman with a long gold scarf and his blade; the four bosses are huge stickmen too. Bases are now giant stone statues of a stickman raising a blade on a pedestal (player grey stone + gold, enemy dark stone + red): they crack in three stages as HP drops and crumble into rubble with dust when destroyed; units spawn from behind their statue. The old floating base HP bars are gone (base HP lives in the top-right panel). Camera is wider and flatter (about 35 m of lane, units ~11-13% of screen height, horizon near the middle, follows the hero; wheel zoom, right-drag tilt, C resets). Flat cartoon backdrop kept (sky, sun, clouds, three hill layers, scenery props from Codex, grassland / desert / lava chapters). Round buttons show the new stickman icons. Raw Codex sheets are in `art_src/raw/` and `art_src/raw_bg/`; rebake with `tools/bake_sprites.gd`. Earlier art styles are archived in `art_src/old_ink/` and `art_src/old_cartoon/`. Debug: `INK_AUTOSHOT=1` plus optional `INK_LEVEL`, `INK_ZOOM`, `INK_HERO_X`, `INK_DAMAGE=<fraction>` and `INK_CRUMBLE=1` for screenshots.
- **Boss Raid part 1: menu, arena, hero movement.** The game now opens on a main menu (`scenes/menu.tscn`) with "战役 Campaign" (the unchanged 2D game) and "3D 首领突袭 Boss Raid". The raid (`scenes/raid.tscn`, `scripts/raid*.gd`) has a round ~60 m toon arena (outlined walls, 6 pillars with colliders, ring of hills, cartoon sky; `scripts/toon.gd` = unshaded 3-band toon shader + inverted-hull outlines, no real-time shadows) and the 3D hero (`hero_anim.glb`) with third-person camera: WASD moves, mouse looks (click to capture, Esc to release), scroll zooms, the camera pulls in when a pillar blocks the view. Test: F5, click the Boss Raid button, walk around. Dev: `RAID_SHOT=<png>` saves a screenshot; `scenes/dev_raid_smoke.tscn` is the headless raid test.
- **Boss Raid part 2: combat.** Left click = 3-hit combo (22 / 26 / 44 damage, wide arc on the finisher, queue the next click during a swing), **Space** = dodge roll (0.5 s, 0.45 s invincible), **Q E R F** = the campaign skills (Slash, Dash, Rally, Sword Rain) with their cooldowns; the hero auto-aims at a boss within 9 m. Hero HP 300 with hit flash, damage numbers and screen flash. Red ground telegraphs (`scripts/telegraph.gd`: circle, line, cone, expanding shockwave ring) fill up and then strike. HUD (`scripts/raid_hud.gd`): hero HP, round skill buttons with cooldown pies, and on touch devices a left joystick plus attack / dodge buttons (drag the right side to look, pinch to zoom; F10 toggles the touch controls on a PC). Right now the arena holds a training dummy that throws telegraphs at you so you can practise; the boss ladder replaces it next. Test: Boss Raid -> click to capture the mouse -> fight the post and roll through its red circles.
- **Boss Raid part 3: boss framework + boss 1, 赤红守卫 Crimson Warden.** The raid is now the boss ladder: 3-2-1 countdown, fight with a timer, then a victory panel (time taken, best time, Next Boss) or a defeat panel (Retry); best clear times per boss are saved in `user://raid.cfg` (`data/raid.gd`). Boss base (`scripts/raid_boss.gd`): chases the hero, picks attacks (each shows a red telegraph first), big HP bar with Chinese + English name on top, phase 2 at 50% HP (roar, +28% speed, shorter warnings, a new attack). The Warden has Slash (cone), Slam (circle) and Charge (line) and gains Whirlwind in phase 2. Debug keys in editor runs: 1-5 jump to a boss. Dev env vars: `RAID_BOSS=<n>`, `RAID_SHOT=<png>`, `RAID_WAIT`, `RAID_BOT=1` (auto-fighting hero), `RAID_LOOK=boss`, `RAID_TRAIN=1` (training dummy).
